# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CS2 players reviewing local match recordings and comparing player or team patterns across demos.

## Product Purpose

An offline desktop-hosted viewer with match replay, map analysis, and match/player statistics. Recordings and parsed caches stay on the user's computer.

## Design Direction

Preserve the existing dark, compact analysis workspace. The map analysis page is the reference for the shared top navigation, as requested by the user. Keep the same brand, navigation order, dimensions, and selected state across all three modes.

## Design Principles

- Keep navigation consistent while keeping each mode's tools accessible.
- Preserve current recording context and existing controls during layout changes.
- Share navigation styles so individual pages cannot drift independently.
- Keep navigation usable at desktop and narrow viewport widths.

## Accessibility

Preserve semantic navigation labels, current-page indication, keyboard focus, and the accessible names and states of existing controls.
